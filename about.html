About The Green Man Works

A Utah County outfit built around the load that's too big for the truck bed and too small for a 20-yard roll-off.

Our story

Small loads, done without the runaround

Most dumpster companies are built around big steel roll-offs and a ticking rental clock. That works fine if you're gutting a house. It's a bad deal if you're cleaning out a garage, tearing out one bathroom, or hauling off a weekend's worth of branches.

So we built the other thing. The Green Man Works rents 3-yard dumpster bags across Utah County — Orem, Provo, Lehi and beyond. The bag shows up folded, opens in seconds, and sits right in your driveway. No walls to heave debris over. No truck backing across your lawn. And because it's on your driveway instead of the street, there's no city permit to chase down.

Fill it this weekend. Fill it next month. There's no rental clock and no late fee — when it's full, you call and the bag disappears.

Why the no-deadline thing matters

Every homeowner who's ever rented a dumpster knows the feeling: the bin lands, and suddenly the whole project is on a timer someone else set. We don't run it that way. The bag is yours until you're done with it. Work at the speed the project actually moves — evenings, weekends, whenever you get to it — and call us when it's ready to go.

Contractors use it a little differently. Folded bags stow on the work truck, so a crew can open one on site, fill it as the job goes, and call for the pickup instead of burning half a day on a transfer-station run. Extra bags at the same location are priced lower, so it scales with the job.

And if you'd rather not touch it at all

Not every pile belongs in a bag. We also do junk removal — you tell us what you've got and where it sits, and we'll get you a price to come haul it off. Send a photo by text or email and we'll take it from there.

What you get either way

A local phone number that a person answers, straight pricing with the whole list on the site, and a truck that shows up when it says it will. That's the entire pitch.
